# Privacy, tracking and retention

Campaign CSV addresses are used only for that campaign. Upload rows are normalized, deduplicated and stored in a campaign-owned snapshot; they never populate a reusable address book. An administrator can replace or delete the upload before approval. Approved audience, delivery, unsubscribe, failure, tracking and audit evidence must be retained according to operational policy and legal requirements. Removing a campaign's delivery evidence by direct SQL is unsupported.

Global suppression prevents sends to an address across campaigns, including case variants. Unsubscribe links are scoped, signed and expire after one year; a completed unsubscribe adds a global suppression that remains effective after the link expires. Failed SMTP deliveries are recorded as failures. Light has no automatic provider bounce or complaint intake; an administrator can add a manual global suppression with either reason. Open tracking is a request for a tiny image and may be inaccurate because of client blocking or proxying. Click tracking records requests for links. Administrators can control tracking under privacy settings and must provide recipients with an appropriate notice and lawful basis. CSV and RSVP exports must guard against spreadsheet formulas and should be stored only in protected locations.

Backups contain personal data and encrypted secrets. They remain encrypted at rest, must be transferred only to an approved protected destination and need retention and restore-drill controls. Logs omit addresses and credentials by default. This project ships only reserved example domains and synthetic data.
